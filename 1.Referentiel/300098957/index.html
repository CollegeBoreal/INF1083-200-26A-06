Robotics Timeline

3000 BCE

Ancient Automatons

Early automated figures appeared in Egyptian water clocks, demonstrating humanity's first attempts at mechanical automation.

400 BCE

Archytas' Mechanical Bird

Greek inventor Archytas reportedly built a steam-powered flying pigeon, one of the earliest autonomous machines.

1495

Leonardo da Vinci

Designed a mechanical knight capable of sitting, moving arms, and raising its visor.

1920

The Word "Robot"

Czech writer Karel Čapek introduced the term "robot" in his play R.U.R.

1942

Isaac Asimov

Introduced the famous Three Laws of Robotics, influencing discussions on robot ethics.

1954

Unimate

George Devol patented Unimate, the world's first programmable industrial robot.

1961

Factory Automation

Unimate started work at General Motors, revolutionizing manufacturing.

2000s

Modern AI Robotics

Robots gained machine vision, artificial intelligence, and autonomous decision-making capabilities.

Today

Humanoid Era

Advanced humanoid robots assist in healthcare, logistics, research, and everyday tasks.
